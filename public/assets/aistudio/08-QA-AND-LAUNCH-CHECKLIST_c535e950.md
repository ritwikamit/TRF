# QA and Launch Checklist

## Functional
All routes load directly; navigation works; logo is crisp; portfolio/lightbox works; videos use poster/click-to-play; contact, map and social links are verified; form validation and success/error states work; no placeholders or fake claims.

## Visual/responsive
Test 320, 375, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440+ CSS px. Check no overflow, legible type, restrained gold accents, correct image crops and mobile gallery behavior.

## Accessibility
Keyboard-only navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, alt text, accessible lightbox, contrast and reduced-motion behavior.

## Performance/security/SEO
Optimize images; lazy-load below fold; avoid multiple videos on first paint; test metadata, canonical, sitemap, robots, Open Graph, dependency audit, secret scan, form abuse protection, HTTPS and headers.

## Launch blockers
Confirm contact/address, service scope, establishment year, asset permissions, testimonials, privacy behavior and form recipient.

## Bug report
ID/date, device/browser, page, reproduction steps, expected/actual result, screenshot, severity, fix and retest status.
